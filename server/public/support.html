Support

Igo5 — the game of Go, in the iPhone app and here in your browser. Both are the same service: one account, one server, the same games.

Something wrong, or something missing?
Write to support@igo5.online. Say which device you are on and what you were doing, and you will get a reply.

I've forgotten my password

Igo5 never asked for your email, so it cannot send you a reset link. Instead, you were given a recovery code when you registered. On the sign-in screen, choose Forgot password, enter your username and that code, and you can set a new password. Each code works once, and using one gives you the next.

Lost the code as well? Write to the address above — but bear in mind that without either the password or the code there is no way to prove the account is yours, so it cannot be recovered.

I'm not getting notifications

iPhone app only — the website doesn't send any. Check that notifications are allowed for Igo5 in the Settings app, and that you are signed in — notifications are tied to your account, not just the device. They only ever tell you that it is your turn, or that an invitation arrived.

Someone keeps inviting me to games

In the iPhone app: open their profile and tap Block, or use Block on the invitation itself. They can no longer invite you, and they are not told. Everyone you have blocked is listed on your own profile, where tapping a name undoes it. Blocking takes effect everywhere, including on the website, but can only be set up in the app for now.

Either way, you can turn off Accept invitations on your profile to stop invitations from everyone at once.

Reporting a name, a title or a tag

In the iPhone app: open the person's profile and tap Report, or use Report on an invitation. Say what the problem is — an offensive name, something written on a game, or how somebody behaved — and add anything else we should know. You can block them in the same step, and we suggest it by default.

Reports are read within one day. Anything offensive is removed and the account behind it is suspended or deleted. You can also write to support@igo5.online instead, which reaches the same person.

Playing without an account

iPhone app only. You can play on one device — both players on the same phone, or against someone nearby over a local connection — without registering at all. Those games are stored on your device rather than on the server.

Deleting your account

Profile, then Delete account — in the app or on the website. It removes your account and the games you played alone. Games against another person stay with them, without your name on them. It cannot be undone.
