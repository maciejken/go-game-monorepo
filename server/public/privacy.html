Privacy Policy

Igo5 · last updated 8 October 2026

Igo5 is a game of Go, played either in the iPhone app or here at igo5.online. Both are the same service — one account, one server, the same games — and this policy covers both. Where something applies to only one of them, it says so.

What follows is everything Igo5 stores about you, which is only what the game needs in order to work.

What is stored

  • Your account — the username you chose, and your password and recovery code stored only as cryptographic hashes. Neither can be read back, by us or by anyone else with access to the database.
  • Your games — every move, pass, and result, together with any title and tags you add, and who you played against.
  • Your settings — your quick game preset, the games you starred, whether you accept invitations, and the list of players you have blocked.
  • A session cookie, so you stay signed in between visits.
  • A notification token — iPhone app only, and only if you allow notifications. An identifier Apple issues for your device, used to tell you when it is your turn.

What is not

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party SDKs in the app. Your email address is never asked for — password recovery uses a code shown once, which is why there is nothing to email you about. Nothing you do here is used to build a profile of you, and nothing is ever sold or shared for marketing.

Who else sees it

  • Other players see your username, and the games you play with them — including any tag you explicitly share onto a shared game. Tags you keep to yourself stay yours.
  • Apple delivers push notifications, and so receives the notification itself and your device token.
  • Our hosting and network providers carry the traffic in the ordinary way a website works, and servers keep standard access logs that include IP addresses. The servers are in the EU.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, from your profile — in the app or on the website. It removes your account, your settings, your notification token, and the games you played alone. Games you played against another person belong to them as well, so those stay — with your name replaced by a marker that identifies nobody. This cannot be undone, and your username becomes free for someone else to take.

Your rights

You can see everything Igo5 holds about you from inside the app or the website, and erase it with the delete button described above. For anything else — a copy of your data, a correction, a complaint — write to support@igo5.online.

Children

Igo5 is not directed at children, and asks for nothing that would identify one.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
